Hi, I am stepping away from my Flathub-related activities after
receiving a series of insulting and belittling remarks from another
team member on Matrix, on the night of July 16, 2026.

<!--more-->

The rude comments were directed by Bart at me and Hub - another
reviewer.

The context is a series of submissions of `com.dygma.bazecor`
[submitted](https://github.com/flathub/flathub/pulls?q=is%3Apr+com.dygma.bazecor)
a few weeks ago. I had not reviewed or touched them but Hub, did. One
of the PRs turned confrontational as the submitter made a rather rude
remark at Hub asking them to find better LLMs to do the reviews.
Subsequently, the submitter was banned by Hub. Shortly after they
probably appealed somewhere as usual and Bart unblocked them, which led
to the third PR at the time of writing.

That one was closed by Hub again as they believed they had blocked the
submitter already and was not aware that they were unbanned. When this
was exchanged on Matrix in #flathub:matrix.org, Bart made a insulting
remark at Hub asking them to "go take a walk", followed by another
insult "I have no energy to keep babysitting you and bbhtt".

Regardless of what I think about the review comments, there wasn't enough there, at least in my view, to justify a ban. If I'd been involved in reviewing the submissions, or if Bart had spoken to us before unblocking them, I would have supported lifting the ban and tried to resolve the matter.

Anyways, at the end of the day, I had nothing to do with all 3 of these submissions which can be seen
on GitHub. I wasn't very active this week due to some IRL stuff and I
wasn't on Matrix for the last 6-7 months. Yet I found myself waking up
to a ping with a drive-by insult directed at me.

None of us here are "babies" and no one employed Bart to be our legal
guardian, so calling it "babysitting" publicly is extremely belittling
and insulting to me and more so when I wasn't even a participant to
anything here. I don't mind random insults thrown at me on the internet
but it hurts when it comes from a person whom I respect somewhat.

I sent a message asking to "go easy on the tone and language" but was
rebuked yet again and the insult justified because apparently someone
somewhere had sent a complaint about me. I am not even sure what
specific complaint was this about as no one said anything to me. Even
if someone somewhere recieved a complaint, I don't think insulting other
people in public view is the correct response to that. I had to reply
in public rather than reaching out privately because the insult at me
was already public and thus the damage done. I don't think privately
bringing it up would have done anything to help here.

I lashed out in frustration after that, saying Bart is not the
"dictator of Flathub", to which I got the reply that they "kinda were"
and a veiled threat thereafter. In retrospect I probably should have
left quietly after sending the first message as things were clear to me
by then. I unfortunately did some of the same things I was arguing
against but it was from a build-up of frustration and I shall apologise
now for that.

However, that comment is factual as Bart has made themselves the
self-appointed BDFL of Flathub, publicly mentioning as such when we
have neither a verbal agreement, nor a formal policy or a structure
appointing anyone in that position. The self-appointed position also
leads them to "override" decisions made by other reviewers or team
members at will without any discussion - be it public or private and on ocassion merge submissions clearly violating documented policies. This
has lead to a number of confrontations over this year, some of which I
linked in my earlier blogposts. So the sentiment expressed was as much
factual as it was made in the heat of the moment.

The broader source of the frustration beyond the specific events here
had been our differing views on AI-generated and low-effort
submissions. Hub and I had become increasingly frustrated by them and
often blocked repeat offenders. While I still believe many of those
actions were justified given how some of these spammers and
slop-operators behaved, there were also cases where I think we were too
harsh or rude, even by our own standards. Bart, on the other hand, had
generally taken a more permissive approach, which I felt was too
permissive. That difference in perspective repeatedly led to
disagreements over moderation and, ultimately, confrontations. While
neither Hub nor me "overrode" each other's moderation desicions, Bart
felt the need to do so on several occassions, without notifying us.
This in turn led to more frustration as in many cases, after being
unblocked some submitters would continue to spam yet again and
so on. People should get the general idea by now. I think if our
behaviour was wrong or someone felt a need to reverse our descisions, it
could have been done through a discussion first. I don't recall any
such discussions initiated with me other than the times we went
confrontational with each other in public view. I'm not sure if I
should have tried to initiate something and in retrospect maybe I
should have done so but in my defence I wasn't the one overriding
someone else's desicion.

Anyways, back to recent events. Shortly after the initial messages on
July 16, Hub had left the matrix room and expressed the intention to
leave Flathub related things in a private Discourse topic made
thereafter.

I know I have made mistakes but I believe I tried to amend them as
much as possible when I could. But the process has to work both ways
because I can't be receiving insults repeatedly. This wasn't the first
time such language was directed at either of us by Bart publicly. I can
tell with full certainty that I had never directed any insult to Bart.

My expectation was that, in the absence of a formal structure,
disagreements between us would be discussed before one person's
decision was overridden. At minimum, I believe the person whose
action is being reversed deserves a message or a ping beforehand. I
have tried to follow this for myself, and I do not recall deliberately
overriding another contributor's decision without first contacting
them. At least it doesn't come to my recent memory.

Those who have access to the private Discourse discussion can see that
I apologised for lashing out on Matrix and tried to carve a path
forward. I guess it was too late and unfortunately, it turned a into
another episode of frustration dump. Also at the time of writing Bart
never replied there. So it appears the effort I put to apologise was
not reciprocated.

I do not believe I can continue collaborating in this environment. So
I stepped away this morning, July 18, removing myself from the GitHub
and every other role I could. I was trying to revoke my SSH access
myself when funnily enough I noticed that it had already been revoked.
I'm not sure what this was about.

Anyway, this brings an end to my 5 year involvement with Flathub and
probably close to 10 years for Hub but I can't speak for them.

Bye.
