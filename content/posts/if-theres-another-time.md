---
title: "If there's another time"
date: 2026-08-16T19:10:19+05:30
---

I loved you so, so much, even when I failed to show it. I watched you
slip away from my own hands. I tried to hide how much it hurt in front
of others but I couldn't bear the pain, and nothing has felt the same
ever since. Maybe I was a coward, I still am, but I couldn't bring
myself to face you in the moments when you needed me the most, so I
chose to cry alone instead. I cried a lot over the years, alone, but the
pain still lingers in me. Now, whenever grief finds me, I think of you,
and I end up crying about everything all over again. It hurts so much
and I want to stop but I can't. I don't know how to let go and if I even
can ever. So I just wanted to say that if there is ever another time
when we can all be together again, I hope I can show you just how much
I love you.
